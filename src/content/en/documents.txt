## Certifying document translations

The type of certification depends on where you will submit the document:

no certification — for personal use and work purposes;
notarised translation — for Ukrainian state institutions, embassies and consulates;
sworn translation — for institutions in Poland;
translation with apostille — for countries party to the Hague Convention.

We arrange certification with a notary ourselves, so you don't need to look for a notary separately. If you are not sure which certification you need, the manager will advise after reviewing the document.

## How we work

### Document review and quote
You send a scan or photo of the document, and the manager calculates the price and turnaround free of charge and advises on the certification you need.

### Translation and editing
The translator translates the document, and the editor checks names, dates, numbers and stamps against the original.

### Notarisation and apostille
If needed, we certify the translation with a notary or a sworn translator, or help with the apostille.

### Collecting the documents
You collect the finished documents at our office in Kyiv. Uncertified translations can be sent electronically.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which certification does my document need?
Ukrainian institutions usually require a notarised translation, Poland requires a sworn translation, and other countries often require an apostille and a notarised translation. It is best to check the exact requirements with the institution where you are submitting the document or with our manager.

### Can several documents be translated in one order?
Yes. Send all the documents together, and we will calculate the total price and turnaround. Translating a package of documents in one order is more convenient and keeps everything consistent, with the same spelling of names.
