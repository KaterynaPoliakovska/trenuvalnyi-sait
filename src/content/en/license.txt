## Why you may need a driving licence translation

### Exchanging a licence abroad
In Poland, to exchange a Ukrainian licence for a Polish one, you need a sworn translation of the Ukrainian driving licence into Polish. Similar requirements apply in other EU countries. → Documents for Poland

### Exchanging a foreign licence in Ukraine

### Car rental and insurance

## Frequently asked questions

### Do both sides of the licence need to be translated?
Yes. The back shows the categories, the dates they were obtained and any restrictions, and institutions only accept a full translation of both sides. So please send photos of both sides of the licence.

### How is a translation different from an international driving permit?
An international driving permit is a separate document issued by a service centre of the Ministry of Internal Affairs. It allows you to drive in many countries but does not replace a translation if an institution specifically requires one. That is why there is no such thing as an «international translation of a driving licence»: to exchange a licence abroad you need an official notarised or sworn translation.
