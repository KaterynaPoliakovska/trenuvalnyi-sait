## Urgent translation

Urgent document translation in Kyiv costs 50 % more than standard translation. An urgent apostille costs from $7 extra. If you need the document on the day you contact us, ask the manager whether this is possible.

## What affects the price of a translation

The price of a document translation depends on:

language — translation from Polish, English or Russian is priced differently;
length — the number of pages or hours;
complexity of the subject — legal, medical and technical texts cost more;
certification — notarised, sworn or apostille;
urgency — urgent translation has a surcharge.
