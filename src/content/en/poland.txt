## Documents needed for Poland

The list of documents depends on the purpose of your trip and the institution. Below are the documents most often translated, but it is worth checking the final requirements with the voivodeship office, university or civil registry office (USC).

### Residence card
For a temporary or permanent residence card, documents issued in Ukrainian that confirm the purpose of stay are translated: birth and marriage certificates (for family reunification), certificates and diplomas. A sworn translation is mandatory for all documents submitted to the voivodeship office that are not in Polish.

### Studies and diploma recognition
For admission to a Polish university, the school certificate or diploma with supplement is translated. Diploma recognition in Poland usually also requires the curriculum. Diplomas are recognised by a Polish university, and school certificates by the regional education board. → Education document translation

### Employment
Employment record book, employment certificates, diplomas and certificates confirming qualifications. → Employment document translation

### Marriage and the civil registry (USC)
To register a marriage at a Polish civil registry office, the birth certificate, a certificate of marital status and, if needed, a certificate of divorce from a previous marriage are translated. → Marriage certificate translation

### Driving licence exchange
To exchange a Ukrainian licence for a Polish one at the district office, you need a sworn translation of the driving licence. → Driving licence translation

We also translate criminal record certificates, passports, medical certificates and tax number certificates if the institution requires them.

## Which translation Polish institutions accept

Polish state institutions, universities and civil registry offices accept sworn translation (tłumaczenie przysięgłe). It is made by a sworn translator of Polish entered in the register of the Polish Ministry of Justice. Such a translation does not need notarisation. A sworn translation has no expiry date: it is valid as long as the document is valid. → Sworn translation

## Is an apostille needed for Poland

It depends on the document. Ukraine and Poland have a legal assistance agreement, so for many civil registry documents (birth and marriage certificates) an apostille is often not required. For university admission and diploma recognition, an apostille on the diploma or school certificate is usually mandatory. The apostille is placed on the original before translation, and then we translate the document together with the apostille. → Apostille

## Frequently asked questions

### Which documents need translating for a residence card?
All documents not in Polish that you submit to the voivodeship office: most often birth and marriage certificates, records and diplomas. The international passport is usually not translated because its details are already in Latin script. The exact list depends on the grounds for the residence card, so send us the list of documents from the voivodeship office and we will tell you what to translate.

### Can all documents for Poland be prepared in one order?
Yes. Send all the documents together, and we will prepare a complete package: apostille, sworn translation and a check of name spelling across all documents. It is faster and more convenient than ordering each document separately.
