## Certifying the passport translation

### Notarised translation
A notarised passport translation in Kyiv is needed for most Ukrainian institutions and embassies. If you need to keep the original passport, we prepare a notarial copy with the translation bound to it. → Notarised translation

### Sworn translation for Poland
A sworn passport translation for Poland is accepted by voivodeship offices and other Polish institutions without a notary. For a residence card, the international passport is often submitted without translation because its details are already in Latin script, but the internal passport or ID card may need to be translated. It is best to check the requirements in advance. → Sworn translation

## Frequently asked questions

### Which passport pages need to be translated?
We translate all completed pages: the holder's details, registration of residence, marital status, information about children and stamps. We don't translate blank pages. An ID card is translated on both sides together with the registration of residence extract. A notarised passport translation has no expiry date: it is valid as long as the passport is valid.

### How is the name transliterated in a passport translation?
We write the name and surname in English, Polish or another language as they appear in the international passport. If there isn't one, we use the official transliteration under the Cabinet of Ministers resolution. Foreign names in translations into Ukrainian are fixed once and used the same way in all your documents to avoid discrepancies.
