## Translation from Ukrainian into Russian

We translate Ukrainian documents into Russian for submission in countries where Russian is an official language: certificates, diplomas, powers of attorney and contracts. ## Certifying the translation

A notarised translation from Russian into Ukrainian is needed for most Ukrainian institutions: administrative centres, civil registry offices, notaries, courts and the Pension Fund. The notary certifies the translator's signature, and the translation acquires legal force. → Notarised translation

## Frequently asked questions

### Do documents issued in Russian need to be translated?
In most cases, yes. Ukrainian state institutions, notaries and courts work in Ukrainian, so a document issued in Russian is usually submitted together with a notarised translation. This also applies to old-style documents issued in Ukraine. It is best to check the exact requirements with the institution where you are submitting the document.
