## Why you may need education documents translated

### Admission to a university abroad
Universities only accept diplomas and certificates with an official translation. For admission in Poland you need a sworn translation of your school certificate with supplement or diploma, and in most cases an apostille on the original.

### Diploma recognition
Recognition (nostrification) is the official recognition of a Ukrainian diploma abroad. We translate the full package for diploma recognition: the diploma, supplement and, if needed, the curriculum.

### Working abroad
Foreign employers ask for a translated diploma to confirm your qualifications. Most often a notarised English translation is needed.

## Certifying the translation

### Notarised translation
A notarised translation of a diploma and supplement is needed for embassies, universities outside Poland and foreign employers. → Notarised translation

### Sworn translation for Poland
Polish universities and education boards accept a sworn translation of a diploma or certificate without additional notarisation. → Sworn translation

### Apostille from the Ministry of Education
The apostille on education documents is placed by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine. It is placed on the original diploma or certificate before translation, and then the document is translated together with the apostille. You can order the apostille and diploma translation together in Kyiv. → Apostille

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the supplement need to be translated together with the diploma?
Yes, in most cases. The diploma itself only confirms the qualification, while universities and recognition bodies assess the subjects, hours and grades from the supplement. Without a translation of the supplement, documents are often not accepted.

### How are subject names and grades translated?
We translate subject names by meaning, taking into account the terminology used in the country of submission. Grades and ECTS credits are given as in the original, without conversion to another system, because conversion is done by the university or recognition body.
