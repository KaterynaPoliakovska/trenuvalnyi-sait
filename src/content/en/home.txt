## How we work

### Document review and quote
You send a scan or photo of the document, and our manager calculates the price and turnaround free of charge and advises on the certification you need.

### Translation and editing
The translator translates the document, and the editor checks names, dates, numbers and stamps against the original.

### Notarisation and apostille
We certify the translation with a notary or sworn translator and help with the apostille if needed.

### Collecting the documents
You collect the finished documents at our office in Kyiv; translations without certification are sent by email.
