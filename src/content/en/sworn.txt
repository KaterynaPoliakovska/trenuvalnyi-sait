## What is sworn translation

Sworn translation (tłumaczenie przysięgłe) is an official translation with legal force in Poland. It is made and certified by a translator with state authorisation, who puts their seal, signature and register number on every page.

### Who is a sworn translator
A sworn translator is a specialist who has passed a state exam and is entered in the register of the Polish Ministry of Justice. Such translators used to be called court translators because they also work for Polish courts. You can check a sworn translator by surname in the public register on the Ministry of Justice website.

### How sworn translation differs from notarised translation
A sworn translation is certified by the translator and accepted by Polish institutions. A notarised translation is certified by a notary who confirms the translator's signature, and it is usually required by institutions in Ukraine. If you are submitting a document in Poland, you need a sworn translation. → More about notarised translation

## Where sworn translation is accepted

Sworn translations are required by Polish state institutions, courts, notaries and universities. They are most often ordered for a residence card: documents in a foreign language submitted to a voivodeship office must be translated into Polish by a sworn translator. Sworn translation is also needed to register a marriage, recognise a diploma, exchange a driving licence and get a job.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a sworn translation be made from a scan?
Yes. A sworn translator can translate a document from a scan and will state in the certification that the translation was made from a copy. Some institutions require a translation from the original, so it is worth checking the requirements in advance.

### Is notarisation needed after a sworn translation?
No. A sworn translation has legal force in Poland without a notary. Notarisation is only needed for institutions that accept notarised translations.

### How long is a sworn translation valid?
The law does not set a validity period for a sworn translation. It is valid as long as the document itself is valid. However, some institutions only accept recently issued documents, such as a criminal record certificate, in which case you will need a translation of the new certificate.
