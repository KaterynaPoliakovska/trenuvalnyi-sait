# Translation for Business in Kyiv

Language House offers corporate translation services for companies and legal entities in Kyiv. We translate contracts, statutory documents, financial statements and technical documentation, arrange interpreting at negotiations and work under contract with or without VAT.

Button: «Get an offer for your company»

## What we translate for companies

### Corporate documents and reporting
Translation of financial statements, audit reports, business plans, presentations and commercial offers into English and Polish. We also translate extracts from the state register, company licences and certificates, tender documentation and documents for opening a branch abroad.

### Contracts and statutory documents
Translation of business contracts, company statutory documents and powers of attorney. We arrange notarised translation of articles of association and apostille for legal entities in one place. → Legal translation

### Technical documentation
Translation of technical documentation for companies: equipment manuals, specifications, drawings and product passports. We compile a glossary for each client so that terms are consistent across all documents.

## Interpreting for business

Interpreters for business meetings, negotiations with foreign partners, conferences and visits to production sites in Kyiv. → Interpreting

## Terms of cooperation

### Contract and NDA
We work under contract and sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving any documents. Your company's confidential information stays protected.

### Invoices with or without VAT
We work with and without VAT, so you can choose the option that suits your accounting.

### Personal manager
Your company gets one manager who knows your documents, deadlines and requirements and is responsible for all orders.

### Discounts for regular clients
Individual discounts apply to regular business clients and large volumes.

## How we work with companies

### Document review and quote
You send the documents online, and the manager prepares a commercial offer with prices and deadlines.

### Translation and editing
A translator specialising in your field translates the documents, and the editor checks terminology, figures and company details.

### Notarisation and apostille
If needed, we certify the translation with a notary and help with the apostille.

### Delivering the translation
Uncertified translations are sent by email in the original format, and certified ones are issued at our office in Kyiv.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can we work under a long-term contract?
Yes. We sign a framework contract, and after that you simply send documents for translation without signing new agreements. Terms, deadlines and prices are fixed in the contract.

### What accounting documents do you provide?
We provide a contract, an invoice and a certificate of completed work, and a tax invoice for VAT payers.
