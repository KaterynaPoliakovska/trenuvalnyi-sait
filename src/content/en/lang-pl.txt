## Frequently asked questions

### Will a translation made in Poland be accepted in Ukraine?
Usually not. Ukrainian institutions require a translation certified by a Ukrainian notary, and a Polish sworn translation into Ukrainian is not accepted everywhere. To avoid refusal, it is better to translate a document issued in Poland in Ukraine with notarisation.

### How are Polish letters in names rendered?
Polish names and surnames with letters such as ą, ę, ł, ś and ż are rendered in Ukrainian according to the rules of practical transcription, for example Łukasz — Лукаш. To avoid discrepancies, we give the original spelling in brackets. Ukrainian names in translations into Polish are written as they appear in the international passport.
