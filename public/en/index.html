How we work

01

Document review and quote

You send a scan or photo of the document, and our manager calculates the price and turnaround free of charge and advises on the certification you need.

02

Translation and editing

The translator translates the document, and the editor checks names, dates, numbers and stamps against the original.

03

Notarisation and apostille

We certify the translation with a notary or sworn translator and help with the apostille if needed.

04

Collecting the documents

You collect the finished documents at our office in Kyiv; translations without certification are sent by email.

Frequently asked questions

What services does Language House offer?

Written, sworn and notarised translation, apostille, interpreting and translation of documents for Poland from and into Polish, English and Russian. The full list is on the «Services» page. All services →

What do I need to get a price quote?

A scan or clear photo of the document and information about where you are submitting it. The manager will calculate the price and turnaround free of charge.

Can I order a translation online?

Yes. Send the document through the form, pay online and collect the certified translation at our office in Kyiv. Online translation →

How long does an urgent translation take?

An urgent translation of a standard document takes 1 working day. The exact time depends on the length and the certification, so the manager will confirm it in the quote.

Can I order an apostille together with the translation?

Yes. We help you obtain the apostille and translate the document together with it, so everything is done in one place. Apostille →
