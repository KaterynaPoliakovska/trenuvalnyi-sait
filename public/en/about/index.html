About Language House Translation Bureau

Language House is a translation bureau in Kyiv specialising in official document translation from and into Polish, English and Russian. We help people and companies prepare documents for Poland, other countries and Ukrainian institutions: from translation to notarisation and apostille.

Our story

Language House was founded in 2015 in Kyiv. We started by translating documents for Ukrainians moving to Poland, which is why Polish and sworn translation are still our main focus. Today we complete more than 2,000 translations a year for private clients and companies.

Our team

Qualified translators

Translators with a degree and specialisation in legal, medical, technical and financial subjects. Every document is assigned to a specialist who knows that field.

Sworn translators

Sworn translators entered in the register of the Polish Ministry of Justice certify translations for Polish institutions.

Project managers

A manager handles each order from the quote to delivery: advises on the certification you need, keeps track of deadlines and answers your questions.

How we ensure translation quality

Editing and proofreading

Every translation is checked by an editor who compares names, dates, numbers and terms with the original. That is why there are no discrepancies in the finished document that could make an institution refuse it.

Legal quality guarantee

We work under contract and are responsible for the accuracy of the translation. If an institution has any comments on the translation, we correct it free of charge.
