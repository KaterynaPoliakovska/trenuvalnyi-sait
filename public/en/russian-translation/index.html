Russian Translation in Kyiv

Language House translates documents from Russian into Ukrainian and from Ukrainian into Russian in Kyiv. Most often we translate old-style documents issued in Russian for submission to Ukrainian institutions, notaries and courts.

Translation from Russian into Ukrainian

Old-style documents

Certificates, diplomas, school certificates and employment record books issued in Russian in Soviet times. Translation of a diploma or employment record book from Russian into Ukrainian is needed for notarial acts, pensions and confirming education or work experience.

Personal documents

Translation of passports, birth certificates and other documents issued in countries where Russian is an official language. Personal document translation →

Legal documents

Translation of legal documents from Russian: contracts, powers of attorney and articles of association, as well as court decisions from Russian into Ukrainian for Ukrainian courts and the enforcement service. Legal translation →

Translation from Ukrainian into Russian

We translate Ukrainian documents into Russian for submission in countries where Russian is an official language: certificates, diplomas, powers of attorney and contracts. If needed, we have the translation notarised and help with an apostille for countries party to the Hague Convention. Apostille →

Certifying the translation

A notarised translation from Russian into Ukrainian is needed for most Ukrainian institutions: administrative centres, civil registry offices, notaries, courts and the Pension Fund. The notary certifies the translator's signature, and the translation acquires legal force. Notarised translation →

Frequently asked questions

Do documents issued in Russian need to be translated?

In most cases, yes. Ukrainian state institutions, notaries and courts work in Ukrainian, so a document issued in Russian is usually submitted together with a notarised translation. This also applies to old-style documents issued in Ukraine. It is best to check the exact requirements with the institution where you are submitting the document.
