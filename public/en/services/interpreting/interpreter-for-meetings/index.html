Interpreter for Meetings in Kyiv

Language House provides an interpreter for meetings in Kyiv: at the notary, the bank, an administrative centre or business negotiations. The interpreter comes to the address you specify, helps you understand every word and sign documents without the risk of misunderstanding.

Book an interpreter

Where the interpreter accompanies you

Notary

An interpreter for a notarial transaction is needed if one of the parties does not speak Ukrainian: when buying property, issuing a power of attorney, signing a marriage contract or handling an inheritance. The notary requires the interpreter to have a document confirming their qualification and an ID, and the interpreter signs the notarial document together with the parties. Our interpreters have all the necessary documents.

Bank

Opening an account, taking out a loan or consulting on banking products for foreigners.

Administrative centres and state institutions

Support at administrative service centres, the migration service, civil registry offices and service centres of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, as well as at court hearings if the interpreter is engaged by a party to the case.

Business meetings

Interpreting for business negotiations, meetings with partners, visits to production sites or showrooms. The interpreter works in consecutive mode, speaking after each segment.

What the service includes

Preparing for the meeting

The interpreter studies the subject, documents and terminology in advance. To prepare for a meeting with an interpreter, send us the agenda, draft contract or presentation — the interpreting will be more accurate.

Interpreting during the meeting

The interpreter accurately conveys the conversation in both directions and helps you understand documents signed on the spot.

Help with documents

If you need a written or notarised translation of documents after the meeting, we will prepare it at our bureau. Document translation →

Languages

We provide interpreters for Polish, English and Russian. We choose an interpreter with experience in the subject of your meeting: legal, financial or technical.
