Legal Translation in Kyiv

Language House provides professional legal translation for companies, lawyers and private clients. We translate contracts, articles of association, powers of attorney and court decisions from and into English, Polish and Russian, preserving the precision of wording on which the legal force of a document depends.

Legal documents we translate

Legal translation is the translation of documents and texts with legal consequences. A mistake in a single term can change the content of an obligation, so these texts are only translated by specialists with legal training.

Contracts and agreements

Supply, lease, sale and service contracts, NDAs and supplementary agreements. We translate both individual contracts and bilingual contracts.

Statutory and registration documents

Articles of association, founding agreements, registry extracts, registration certificates and minutes of general meetings.

Powers of attorney

Powers of attorney for representing interests, selling property or managing assets abroad.

Court decisions and procedural documents

Court decisions and rulings, statements of claim, appeals and enforcement documents for submission to foreign courts.

Translation for international use

For a legal document to be valid abroad, a translation alone is not enough. We handle the whole process, from translation to certification.

Notarisation

The notary certifies the translator's signature, and the translation is accepted by state institutions, embassies and counterparties. Notarised translation →

Apostille

For countries party to the Hague Convention, a document needs an apostille. We help obtain it together with the translation. Apostille →

Who does the legal translation

Translators specialising in law

Legal texts are translated by specialists who know legal language and the differences between legal systems. Translating legal terminology requires more than language skills: the translator chooses an equivalent with the same legal meaning in the country of submission. Clients most often order legal translation into English for international counterparties and courts.

Confidentiality and NDA

Legal documents often contain trade secrets. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before work starts, and only the translator and the manager have access to the documents.

How we work

01

Document review and quote

You send the document online, and the manager calculates the price and turnaround. If needed, we sign an NDA first.

02

Translation and editing

A legal translator translates the document, and the editor checks terminology, company details, amounts and dates.

03

Notarisation and apostille

If needed, we certify the translation with a notary and help with the apostille.

04

Delivering the translation

Uncertified translations are sent electronically in the original format, and certified translations are issued at our office in Kyiv.

Frequently asked questions

Can you translate a bilingual contract?

Yes. We lay out the contract in two columns: the original and the translation side by side, each clause opposite its counterpart. This format is convenient for both parties to sign.

Do you keep the legal terminology of the original?

Yes. For every project we compile a glossary so that each concept is translated the same way throughout the document and in the client's future documents. If your company has its own glossary, we will use it.
