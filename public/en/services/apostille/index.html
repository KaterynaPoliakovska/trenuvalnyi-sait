What is an apostille

An apostille is a special stamp that confirms the authenticity of the signature and seal on an official document. Thanks to it, a Ukrainian document is recognised in countries party to the 1961 Hague Convention without further verification. An apostille looks like a square stamp with the word «Apostille» and a number, or a separate sheet attached to the document.

Which countries require an apostille

An apostille is needed for more than 120 countries party to the Hague Convention, including all EU countries, the USA and the UK. For countries outside the convention, consular legalisation is required instead: a longer procedure through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the embassy of the destination country. Whether documents for Poland need an apostille depends on the document and the institution: it is usually required for university admission, while for some civil registry documents a sworn translation is enough. Where the apostille is placed

Depending on the type of document, the apostille is placed by different ministries.

Ministry of Justice apostille

The Ministry of Justice places the apostille on civil registry documents, court decisions, notarial documents, notarised copies and translations.

Ministry of Education and Science apostille

The Ministry of Education places the apostille on education documents: diplomas, school certificates, supplements and academic transcripts. This is where a diploma gets its apostille in Ukraine.

Ministry of Foreign Affairs apostille

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs places the apostille on documents of other state bodies that are not handled by the Ministry of Justice or the Ministry of Education.

Documents we apostille

Civil registry documents

Apostille on birth, marriage and divorce certificates and registry extracts.

Education documents

Apostille on diplomas and supplements in Kyiv, as well as on school certificates and academic transcripts.

Criminal record certificate

Apostille on a criminal record certificate for work, a visa or a residence card. Because the certificate is valid for a limited time, the apostille is often placed urgently.

Notarial documents

Apostille on notarised powers of attorney, statements, consents and notarised copies. An apostille cannot be placed on an original passport, but it can be placed on a notarised copy of the passport.

We also apostille court decisions, registry extracts and company legal documents.

Apostille together with translation

In most cases a document with an apostille also needs to be translated. We provide apostille with translation into Polish or English, and for Poland — apostille and sworn translation. Frequently asked questions

What comes first: the apostille or the translation?

First the apostille is placed on the original document, and then the document is translated together with the apostille. If the country requires a double apostille, the second one is placed on the notarised translation.

Can an apostille be placed on a copy of a document?

Yes, but only on a notarised copy. The apostille on it is placed by the Ministry of Justice. This is convenient if you need to keep the original in Ukraine. The apostille itself does not expire, but an institution may require a recently issued document, such as a new criminal record certificate.
