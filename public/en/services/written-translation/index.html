Written Translation in Kyiv: Uncertified, Notarised and Sworn

Language House provides professional written translation of texts and documents in Kyiv. We translate technical, marketing and academic texts and business correspondence, and when needed we certify the translation with a notary or a sworn translator.

Get a quote

What written translation includes

Written translation is more than replacing words in one language with words in another. We convey the meaning, style and terminology so that the text reads naturally and is ready to use.

Translating the text

A translator who knows the subject translates the text, keeping the meaning, terms and style of the original.

Editing and proofreading

The editor checks accuracy, grammar, terminology, names and figures. The finished text needs no further work.

Keeping the formatting

We keep the structure of the document: tables, lists, headings and layout. You get the translation in the same format as the original.

Types of written translation

The type of translation depends on what you need the text for.

Uncertified translation

An uncertified translation in Kyiv suits work, study and personal texts when the institution does not require a notary's or sworn translator's seal. It is the fastest and most affordable option.

Notarised translation

Needed to submit documents to Ukrainian state institutions, embassies and consulates. The notary certifies the translator's signature. Notarised translation →

Sworn translation

Needed for Polish institutions: voivodeship offices, courts and universities. This translation is certified by a sworn translator. Sworn translation →

Translation subjects

Technical texts and manuals

Written translation of technical texts in Kyiv: manuals, specifications, technical documentation and equipment passports. The texts are translated by specialists with a technical background.

Marketing texts and websites

Written translation for websites, presentations, advertising materials and product descriptions. We adapt the text for the reader rather than translating word for word.

Academic and educational texts

Articles, dissertations, abstracts, curricula and teaching materials.

Business correspondence

Letters, commercial offers, meeting minutes and internal company documents.

Frequently asked questions

What is a translation page?

A translation page is 1,800 characters with spaces. We count the characters of the original text, so you know the price before work starts.

When is an uncertified translation enough?

An uncertified translation suits work documents, correspondence, websites, manuals and texts for personal use. If the document is submitted to a state institution, embassy or court, notarisation or sworn certification is usually required.

Do you translate PDF files and scans?

Yes, we translate PDF files, scans and photos of documents. The main thing is that the text is clearly visible.
