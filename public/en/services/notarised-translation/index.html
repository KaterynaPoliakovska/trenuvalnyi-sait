What is notarisation of a translation

Notarisation of a translation is official confirmation that the translation was made by a qualified translator. Such a translation has legal force and is accepted by state institutions.

Certification of the translator's signature

The notary certifies not the content of the translation but the authenticity of the signature of a translator with the appropriate qualifications. That is why a notarised translation is made only by a qualified translator, and the bureau is responsible for its accuracy.

Notarised or sworn: which to choose

A notarised translation is needed for Ukrainian institutions and most embassies. For Polish institutions, a sworn translation is required. Sworn translation →

When you need a notarised translation

Ukrainian state institutions

Administrative service centres, civil registry offices, service centres of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the migration service, courts and notaries accept foreign documents only with a notarised translation.

Embassies and consulates

For visas, citizenship and consular services, embassies usually require a notarised translation.

Submission abroad with an apostille

For countries party to the Hague Convention, an apostille is placed on the notarised translation or on the original document. We help arrange everything in one place. Apostille →

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be present at the notary?

No. The notary certifies the translator's signature, not yours, so we handle everything ourselves. All we need from you is the document.

Can the translation of a copy be certified?

Yes. If you need to keep the original, the notary first certifies a copy of the document, and the translation is bound to the notarial copy. The original document is needed for this. The requirements for notarisation are simple: the document must be legible, with all seals and signatures, without corrections or damage.

How much does notarisation of a translation cost?

Notarisation costs from $7 per document and is added to the price of the translation. The manager will give you the exact amount after a free quote.
