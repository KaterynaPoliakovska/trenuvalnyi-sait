Online Document Translation

Language House accepts orders for document translation online: you send a scan or photo, and we calculate the price, translate and certify the document. Unlike an online translator, your document is translated by a professional, and the translation has the same legal force as one ordered at the office.

Frequently asked questions

What quality should the scan be?

The text, seals and signatures must be clearly visible, and the document must be photographed in full, with all corners. A 300 dpi scan is ideal. A phone photo also works if it is not blurred and has no glare.

Is it safe to send documents online?

Yes. Only the manager and the translator have access to your documents, and we sign a non-disclosure agreement if needed. We do not share files with third parties.

When is the original document needed?

A scan is enough for translation. The original is needed if a notary certifies a copy of the document or if the institution requires a translation made from the original. The manager will let you know at the quote stage.
