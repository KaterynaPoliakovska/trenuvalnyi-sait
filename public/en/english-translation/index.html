How we work

Frequently asked questions

British or American English: which should I choose?

Choose based on the country where you are submitting the documents. British and American English differ in spelling (colour / color), date format (day/month or month/day) and some terminology. British English is usually used for the UK, the EU and Australia, and American English for the USA. If you are not sure, tell us the country of submission and we will choose the right variant.
