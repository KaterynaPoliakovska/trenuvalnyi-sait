Employment Document Translation in Kyiv

Language House translates employment documents for working abroad, visas, residence cards and confirming work experience. We translate employment record books, contracts and certificates from and into Polish, English and Russian and certify the translation with a notary or sworn translator.

Employment documents we translate

Employment record book

Translation of the employment record book with all entries on hiring, transfers and dismissal, as well as seals and signatures. We translate paper record books and Pension Fund statements of employment history (forms OK-5 and OK-7), which replace entries in the electronic record book.

Certificates from the employer

Certificates of employment, position, length of service and references from the employer.

Income certificate

Certificates of salary and income for embassies, visa centres and banks.

Employment contract

Translation of an employment contract with a foreign employer into Ukrainian or of a Ukrainian contract into a foreign language. Employment contracts are translated with precise legal terminology: the terms, rights and obligations of the parties must be conveyed without distortion.

Why you may need employment documents translated

Working abroad

Foreign employers and agencies ask for a translated employment record book and certificates to confirm your experience and qualifications.

Confirming work experience and pensions

A translated employment record book is needed to count Ukrainian work experience abroad or foreign work experience in Ukraine. The procedure depends on agreements between countries, so it is worth checking the list of documents with the pension institution.

Visa and residence card

For a work visa and residence card in Poland, you submit a translated employment contract and certificates certified by a sworn translator. Documents for Poland →

Certifying the translation

Notarised translation

A notarised translation of an employment contract or record book is needed for embassies, consulates and Ukrainian institutions. Most foreign employers also accept this certified translation. Notarised translation →

Sworn translation for Poland

Polish voivodeship offices, ZUS and employers accept a sworn translation of employment documents without additional certification. Sworn translation →

Frequently asked questions

Does the whole employment record book need to be translated?

Usually all completed pages are translated: the title page and the employment entries. Some institutions ask only for entries for a certain period, so it is worth checking the requirements in advance. We do not translate blank pages or include them in the price.

How are job titles and seals translated?

We translate job titles by meaning, choosing an equivalent that a foreign employer will understand. The text of seals and stamps is translated in full and marked in square brackets, for example: [seal: …]. This way the institution can see that the translation matches the original.
