Why you may need a marriage certificate translation

Recognition of marriage abroad

For a marriage registered in Ukraine to be recognised in another country, the certificate is submitted with an official translation. The most common order is a marriage certificate translation into English for embassies and visa centres.

Change of surname in documents

The translated certificate confirms the new surname when replacing documents, opening a bank account or arranging insurance abroad.

Residence card and family reunification

To submit documents in Poland, you need a translation of the marriage certificate into Polish certified by a sworn translator. Documents for Poland →

Certifying the translation

The type of certification for a marriage certificate translation depends on the country and the institution where you submit it.

Notarised translation

A notarised translation is needed for embassies, consulates and Ukrainian institutions. Notarised translation →

Sworn translation for Poland

Polish institutions accept a sworn translation without additional notarisation. Sworn translation →

With apostille

For countries party to the Hague Convention, an apostille is placed on the marriage certificate. It is usually placed on the original, and then the document is translated together with the apostille. Some institutions also require an apostille on the notarised translation, so it is worth checking the order of steps with the institution. Apostille →

Frequently asked questions

How is the surname after marriage translated?

We write the surnames before and after marriage as they appear in the spouses' international passports. If there is no new passport yet, we use the official transliteration. That is why we recommend sending copies of international passports together with the certificate, so that surnames are spelled the same in all documents.

Can you translate a marriage certificate issued abroad?

Yes. We translate marriage certificates from English, Polish and Russian into Ukrainian for Ukrainian institutions, for example to register the marriage in Ukraine or change the surname in a passport.
